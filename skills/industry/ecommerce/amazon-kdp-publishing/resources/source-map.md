## Recheck-before-publishing list

For any KDP project, **always verify the live page** for these eight fields before locking:

1. **Paperback royalty threshold** (currently $9.99 on Amazon.com; varies per marketplace). `[HIGH volatility]`
2. **Kindle 70% royalty range** (currently $2.99–$9.99 on Amazon.com; varies per marketplace). `[HIGH volatility]`
3. **Kindle delivery cost per MB** (currently $0.15 on Amazon.com). `[HIGH volatility]`
4. **Print fixed cost + per-page cost** (currently $1.00 + $0.012/page for paperback B&W on US). `[HIGH volatility]`
5. **Page-count ranges per trim + paper + ink** combination. `[HIGH volatility]`
6. **KDP Select enrollment terms and KENP fund rate** (changes monthly). `[HIGH volatility]`
7. **Low-content book policies** (KDP periodically tightens these). `[HIGH volatility]`
8. **Supported manuscript formats per language** (especially for RTL and CJK scripts). `[HIGH volatility]`

If your decision rests on any of these, open the source URL and check the current value before publishing.
