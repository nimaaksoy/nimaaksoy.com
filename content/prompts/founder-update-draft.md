Turn these rough notes into a clear founder update.

Audience:
[INVESTORS / ADVISORS / INTERNAL TEAM]

Tone:
Direct, calm, specific, no hype.

Notes:
[PASTE NOTES]

Structure:
1. One-sentence summary
2. What changed since the last update
3. Metrics that matter
4. What is working
5. What is not working
6. Current priorities
7. Specific asks

Rules:
- Keep it under 500 words
- Use bullets where they improve scanning
- Do not hide bad news
- Make every ask easy to answer
