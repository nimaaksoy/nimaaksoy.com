Create an 8-second ultra-realistic luxury skincare commercial featuring a premium pink-and-white cosmetic cream jar placed at the center of a glossy reflective surface. Elegant white silk fabric flows gracefully around the product with realistic physics, while delicate pink mist, floating dust particles, and soft cinematic light rays create a dreamy, luxurious atmosphere.

The camera begins with an extreme macro close-up of the jar, revealing its premium textures and glossy finish. It then performs a slow, smooth 360-degree cinematic orbit around the product before gently pushing in to perfectly focus on the product label. Camera movement should be fluid, stable, and premium, resembling a high-end luxury beauty commercial.

The background features a soft pastel pink gradient with subtle bokeh, glossy reflections, and professional studio lighting. Use realistic HDR illumination, soft rim lighting, gentle highlights, accurate reflections, and premium cinematic composition. Maintain a shallow depth of field with seamless focus transitions to emphasize the elegance of the product.

Photorealistic textures, ultra-detailed materials, realistic lighting, cinematic atmosphere, smooth animation, luxury cosmetic advertising style, premium beauty brand aesthetic, 8K resolution, 24fps, commercial-quality rendering.

No people, no hands, no text, no logos, no watermark, no extra objects—only the cosmetic cream jar displayed in a clean, elegant luxury studio environment.
