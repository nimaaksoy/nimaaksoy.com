Analyze the uploaded reference photo first. Preserve the person's exact facial identity, body proportions, hairstyle, skin tone, clothing, pose, camera angle, perspective, lighting, shadows, and composition without changing anything. Keep the same clean off-white wall and minimal studio-style editorial background with soft natural light and premium fashion photography aesthetics. Place a life-sized black ink hand-drawn doodle clone beside the real person. The doodle must clearly be the same person, wearing the same outfit, but with a fun, playful, expressive personality—dancing, singing, celebrating, posing dramatically, jumping, taking a selfie, holding a coffee, or striking energetic fashion poses as if it represents the person's imagination coming to life. The real person stays calm and realistic while only the doodle is animated. Add a few subtle sketch elements like hearts, stars, and motion lines around the doodle only. Blend the illustration naturally into the photo for a luxury fashion editorial look. Do not crop, replace, beautify, stylize, or alter the real person in any way. Do not add any reference thumbnail or inset image. Ultra-realistic, premium, clean, minimalist, 4:5 vertical.
