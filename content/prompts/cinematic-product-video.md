Create a cinematic product video for [PRODUCT].

Style:
- Premium studio lighting
- Slow, controlled camera movement
- High contrast but realistic shadows
- Macro details before the full reveal

Scene:
Start with a close-up of one distinctive material or detail. Move slowly around the product as light reveals the shape. End on a clean hero shot with the product centered and enough negative space for a short headline.

Camera:
Use a 70mm lens feel, shallow depth of field, smooth dolly movement, and no handheld shake.

Output:
Keep the video elegant, minimal, and useful for a product launch page.
