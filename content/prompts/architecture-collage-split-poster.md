Convert each uploaded photo into an independent luxury art poster per photo. Do not make collages of multiple photos.

The complete set will be in vertical 3:4 format, strictly divided 50:50 top and bottom.

The upper half will use the original photo faithfully, without altering the subject, terrain, architecture, texture, light, color, or positional relationships, adding only a delicate tone as in architecture magazines or photographic exhibitions, and a very subtle film grain. If necessary, you can naturally extend the sky or surrounding environment, but without distorting, moving, or changing the main subject.

The lower half will be recomposed over a warm ivory paper background, recreating the same landscape as an "architectural collage + silkscreen + design blueprint." Do not use simple geometric illustrations; express the terrain, buildings, plants, paths, steps, etc., extracted from the photo, through translucent color planes, overlapping rectangles, fine technical drawing lines, and partially preserved photographic textures. At the bottom, leave an echo with elongated faint fragments and fine lines that dissolve downward.

The color palette will focus on deep green, teal green, olive, sage, and charcoal extracted from the photo, preserving a single characteristic color as an accent in a specific place.

In the upper left corner of the lower half, place a 2-3 word English title representing the photo, in a delicate serif typeface with wide letter spacing. Below it, the number (e.g., No. 01)», and in the upper right corner, the year in small text.

Design with the serenity and territorial rootedness of an international architecture studio, a contemporary art exhibition, or a high-quality travel magazine. Avoid simple mountain logos, bland flat illustrations, generic landscapes, childish styles, 3D, cybernetic expressions, cheap template vibes, unnecessary texts, or logos.
