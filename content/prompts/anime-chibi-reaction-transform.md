Create a 15-second anime character transformation showcase on a pure white seamless studio background. Use one single anime character throughout with perfectly consistent face, hairstyle, outfit, body proportions and colors. Keep the camera mostly static in a clean full-body or 3/4 studio framing.

Show the character in a normal anime form only at the beginning. The character smiles at the camera and gives a small friendly wave. After that, the character must never return to the normal form. Each new emotional state should morph directly into the next one in one continuous transformation chain.

0-1s
Show the character in their normal anime form for the first and only time. They smile naturally at the camera, give a small friendly wave and hold a simple clean pose with subtle breathing.

1-3s
The character suddenly transforms into an exaggerated super-deformed goofy form with loose limbs, asymmetrical eyes, a ridiculous grin and playful bouncing movement. Large kinetic typography appears: "SILLY". The letters wobble and bounce with the character.

3-5s
Without returning to normal, the silly form morphs directly into a tiny shy chibi form. Shoulders raised, knees turned inward, intense blush, nervous fingers and avoiding eye contact. Typography appears: "SHY". The letters softly shrink and squeeze inward around the character.

5-7s
The shy form morphs directly into an extreme shocked reaction. Huge eyes, tiny pupils, wide-open mouth, rigid body and explosive comic speed lines. Typography appears: "SHOCKED!" and rapidly expands outward like a visual explosion.

7-9s
The shocked form transforms directly into a fiery angry chibi form. Clenched fists, puffed cheeks, shaking body, stomping feet and a classic anime anger symbol on the forehead. Typography appears: "ANGRY" and slams heavily into frame.

9-11s
The angry form morphs directly into a frantic tiny chibi version. Arms flailing, sweat drops flying everywhere, legs scrambling rapidly in place and chaotic motion trails surrounding the body. Typography appears: "PANIC!" and shakes, scatters and struggles to stay aligned.

11-13s
The panic form transforms directly into a simplified smug caricature. Half-lidded eyes, tiny confident smile, raised chin and exaggerated proud posture. Typography appears: "SMUG" and slides smoothly into place.

13-15s
The smug form morphs directly into a monochrome devastated chibi form collapsed on the floor. Empty eyes, slumped posture and a tiny ghost-like soul slowly floating out of the body. Typography appears: "DONE." and drops downward, loses energy and deflates beside them. Hold the final defeated pose.

Use sharp snap transitions, squash-and-stretch, proportion morphing, smear frames, comic symbols and expressive kinetic typography. Each emotional form should have its own distinct animation language. Fast, playful, polished and highly readable, like an anime reaction transformation reel.
