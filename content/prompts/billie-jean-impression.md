SUBJECTS
17-year-old boy inspired by Michael Jackson @ image1. Ultra short black haircut with clean tapered sides and a small amount of naturally falling micro-curled fringe at the front. Wearing a classic black sequined performance outfit, white dress shirt, cropped trousers, white glitter socks, and black leather shoes. Wearing a black rolled-brim fedora.

ENVIRONMENT
Dark stage environment with a single cold white spotlight shining down onto the center of the stage. Black floor with slight reflections, surrounding environment kept dim.

MUSIC
Use Michael Jackson’s “Billie Jean” as the background music.

STYLE
Digital painting aesthetic. Large simplified color blocks, hard-edge brush strokes, low-detail structure, heavy shadows, visible hand-painted texture. No realistic 3D look, no commercial CG advertisement look, no polished realism.

SHOT 1
Medium close-up shot. The boy stands in the center of the spotlight, one hand lowering the brim of the fedora while his body subtly sways with the music. He then slowly raises his head to look at the camera and pauses. Slight shoe friction sounds and music drum beats can be heard on stage.

SHOT 2
Wide shot. The boy continuously performs Michael Jackson “Billie Jean” style movements in the center of the stage, including body leans, hip movements, footwork changes, and brief pauses. The black sequined jacket constantly reflects under the lighting.

SHOT 3
Medium shot. The boy quickly turns around, followed by continuous shoulder movements, foot taps, and classic MJ hand gestures. The sequined jacket keeps shimmering with the movement. “Billie Jean” continues playing in the background.

SHOT 4
Low-angle wide shot. The boy performs continuous gliding steps, spins, and moonwalk movements, moving backward across the stage. The white glitter socks stand out strongly against the dark stage. The spotlight remains focused around the character throughout.

SHOT 5
Medium close-up shot. After the final heavy beat of the music lands, the boy points one foot forward, slightly tilts his body sideways, one hand holding the fedora while the other hand wearing the white glove hangs naturally downward. The character remains standing at the center of the stage. The spotlight only illuminates the outline of the character while the background gradually becomes quiet.
