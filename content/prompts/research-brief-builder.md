Build a research brief from the material below.

Topic:
[TOPIC]

Material:
[PASTE NOTES, LINKS, QUOTES, OBSERVATIONS]

Output:
- Executive summary
- What we know
- What is uncertain
- Key evidence
- Contradictions or weak assumptions
- Opportunities
- Risks
- Recommended next questions

Constraints:
- Separate facts from interpretation
- Do not overstate confidence
- Use plain language
- Keep the final brief useful for a decision maker
