Create an ultra-realistic 3×3 editorial lifestyle collage featuring the same mature male portrayed from the uploaded photo in nine different imaginative professions, hobbies, and dream scenarios. Each panel should seamlessly blend real photography with hand-drawn black ink doodle illustrations, creating the illusion that simple scratch art comes to life around the subject.

The collage should have a minimalist white studio background with soft diffused lighting and subtle shadows. Every frame is separated by thin white borders, maintaining a clean magazine-style grid layout. The photographic subject remains perfectly consistent across all nine scenes with identical facial identity, skin tone, hairstyle, and natural proportions.

Character consistency
- Same adult male in every frame
- Happy face
- Natural facial proportions
- Consistent identity throughout
- Professional DSLR quality, sharp focus
- 8K ultra-high resolution
- Photorealistic rendering

Panel 1 — Surfing adventure
The subject rides a surfboard in an energetic surfing pose. Illustrated black ink waves, spray, birds, and clouds surround him as expressive sketches.

Panel 2 — Astronaut in space
The subject wears a realistic NASA-inspired astronaut suit, floating weightlessly in space with a launching rocket beneath him. His body drifts naturally in zero gravity among stars, planets, and sketch-line cosmos doodles.

Panel 3 — Reading on clouds
The subject sits comfortably on fluffy white clouds reading a book, with cozy study-room doodles: bookshelf, window, lamp, and a small side table with coffee. The clouds blend realistically with the illustrated environment.

Panel 4 — Scientist
The subject wears a clean white laboratory coat and safety goggles while examining a glass test tube. Around him: molecular structures, chemical formulas, flasks, bubbles, and a comic-style speech bubble saying “EUREKA!”

Panel 5 — Gardener
The subject kneels beside a raised wooden garden bed watering fresh vegetables with a metal watering can. Illustrated herbs, leaves, flowers, insects, butterflies, and botanical sketches surround the realistic garden.

Panel 6 — Meditation
The subject sits cross-legged in meditation on a large illustrated lotus flower, with glowing aura circles, sparkles, and pen-inspired line work behind him, creating a tranquil atmosphere.

Panel 7 — Chef
The subject wears a chef’s coat and holds fresh produce (tomatoes, zucchini) near a pot. Kitchen doodles: shelves, utensils, steam curls, herbs, and cooking tags surround the scene.

Panel 8 — Carpenter
The subject wears protective gloves and safety glasses while carefully building with a hammer at a realistic workbench. Additional rulers, screws, wood scraps, and tool doodles fill the background.

Panel 9 — Painter
The subject wears a paint-splattered artist apron while holding a paintbrush at an easel with a partial landscape. Hand-drawn paint splashes, brushes, tubes, creative doodles, and artistic sketch lines surround him.

Artistic style
- Hyper-realistic photography combined with monochrome hand-drawn doodle illustration
- Seamless interaction between the real subject and sketch art
- Clean white seamless background
- Soft high-key studio lighting

- Balanced composition
