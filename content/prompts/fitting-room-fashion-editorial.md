Create a 9:16 fashion editorial poster built around a real dressing-room moment.

Scene: [fitting room / mirror check / quick change / backstage / walk test]
Fashion action: [describe the natural dressing or styling action]
Outfit: [describe the full look]
Accent color: [red / orange / cobalt blue / magenta / yellow / green / purple]
Headline: [short fashion-related title]

Use realistic black-and-white documentary photography as the main visual. The model should be naturally interacting with the clothing — adjusting, changing, checking the fit, walking, or preparing to step out — not posing directly for the camera.

Combine the monochrome photo with one single highly saturated accent color, warm off-white paper background, strong negative space, and oversized condensed sans-serif typography.

Let the typography interact with the subject through cropping and layering, but keep all headline letters clean, solid, sharp, and fully rendered — no distressed, dirty, scratched, or broken text.

Add only a few meaningful editorial details such as time, fitting-room status, look name, or a short phrase. Keep the layout bold, asymmetric, minimal, and fashion-magazine driven.

Style: Swiss editorial design × brutalist typography × fashion documentary photography × modern print poster.

Avoid multiple accent colors, random decorative text, excessive graphic symbols, messy typography, glossy commercial advertising, stiff model poses, CGI skin, and cluttered layouts.
