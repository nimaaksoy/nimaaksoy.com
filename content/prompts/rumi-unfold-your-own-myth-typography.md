Create a visually stunning 15-second, 16:9 cinematic typography video built around this exact Rumi quote:

“Don't be satisfied with stories, how things have gone with others. Unfold your own myth.”

CONCEPT
Begin inside stories inherited from other people: closed frames, repeated paths and blank manuscript pages. Gradually break away from those fixed narratives and unfold an entirely new world.

Combine deep lapis blue, aged ivory paper, black ink, muted copper and luminous gold. Use elegant editorial design influenced by illuminated manuscripts, geometric architecture and folding paper, but avoid imitation Middle Eastern writing or decorative pseudo-text.

TYPOGRAPHY
Display only the exact quote phrases and final attribution specified below. Do not add book titles, handwritten notes, symbols or invented language.

Use refined serif typography with small uppercase accents. Keep every phrase correctly spelled, sharp and readable. Give each phrase one primary animation. Once formed, the letters must become solid and motionless.

0–3.5s:
Begin with a closed ivory manuscript floating in a vast lapis-blue space. It opens to perfectly blank pages. A band of copper light passes across the paper, revealing:

DON'T BE SATISFIED
WITH STORIES,

The words appear through a controlled page-turn mask. Hold the completed phrase clearly.

3.5–7s:
The page divides into a corridor of repeating rectangular frames. Inside each frame, the same small anonymous silhouette walks along the same circular path. The frames become progressively smaller toward the center.

Reveal:
HOW THINGS HAVE GONE
WITH OTHERS.

The words travel across the repeating frames, then detach from them and settle as stable ivory typography in the foreground.

7–10.5s:
The corridor stops. One blank sheet separates from the repeating pages and floats toward the camera. It unfolds along crisp geometric creases, expanding into an impossible landscape of paper mountains, stairways, arches and a golden horizon.

Across the opening landscape, reveal:

UNFOLD
YOUR OWN MYTH.

“UNFOLD” opens horizontally like a folded strip of paper. “YOUR OWN” remains small and centered. “MYTH” rises in monumental gold letters, then becomes completely still.

10.5–12s:
No new text. The paper landscape continues unfolding beyond the frame, transforming into a vast night sky. Gold paths become constellations while the original repeating frames collapse into a single distant point.

12–15s:
Resolve to a clean lapis-blue background crossed by one thin gold fold line. Display the complete quote in centered ivory serif typography:

“Don't be satisfied with stories,
how things have gone with others.
Unfold your own myth.”

Set “Unfold your own myth” in gold. Place RUMI below in small uppercase letters. Hold the final composition perfectly still and readable through the end.

If timing compresses, remove secondary landscape details rather than shortening the final quote.

AUDIO
Begin with paper movement, a quiet room tone and one low plucked string. Add a restrained rhythmic pulse as the repeating frames appear. Introduce wind, resonant strings and widening harmonics as the new world unfolds. End with one warm strike and a sustained open chord. No fantasy choir, aggressive percussion or recognizable existing melody.
